# ALKOTA PERFORMANCE ENGINEERING

> **PERFORMANCE ENGINEERING**  
> High-performance mountain bicycle engineering platform & digital experience.

---

## ABOUT ALKOTA
ALKOTA Performance Engineering exists around one central belief: **Performance is engineered.**

This application represents the digital storefront and engineering showcase for ALKOTA's flagship development bike platform, **PROJECT 01**.

---

## TECH STACK
- **Framework**: [Next.js 15](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router, Server Components)
- **Language**: [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) (Strict configuration)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) & Custom CSS Design Tokens
- **Animation**: [Motion for React](https://motion.dev/)
- **3D Engine**: [React Three Fiber](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/) & [Three.js](https://threejs.org/)
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)

---

## ARCHITECTURE & ROUTES

### Public Navigation
- `/` — Flagship Homepage (12-section cinematic narrative)
- `/bikes` & `/bikes/project-01` — Platform overview & detail page
- `/configure` — Interactive 3D & 2D Configurator Application
- `/engineering` — Engineering Centre Hub
  - `/engineering/chassis` — Frame structure & packaging
  - `/engineering/kinematics` — Interactive leverage & anti-squat telemetry
  - `/engineering/materials` — Carbon composite & metal alloy explorer
  - `/engineering/testing` — Validation laboratory & fatigue testing
- `/journal` & `/journal/[slug]` — Field Notes editorial
- `/about` — Origin narrative & engineering philosophy
- `/support` — Owner portal, technical guides, warranty
- `/dealers` — Performance partner locator
- `/contact` — Engineering inquiry form

### Developer Route
- `/design-system` — Design tokens, typography test, technical labels, cards, and theme switcher preview.
